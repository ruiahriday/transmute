TRANSMUTE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD: HOW TO INSTALL
=============================================

You need: a Mac, the free Xcode app, your normal Apple ID, and a cable for your
iPhone or iPad (iOS / iPadOS 16 or newer). No paid developer account is needed
to put it on your own devices.

ONE-TIME SETUP ON THE MAC
1. Install Xcode from the Mac App Store (free, but a big download).
2. Open Xcode once. If it asks which platforms to install, tick iOS and click
   Download & Install. Wait for it to finish.
3. In Xcode, open the Xcode menu > Settings > Accounts. Click + (bottom left),
   choose Apple ID, and sign in with your usual Apple ID.

OPEN THE PROJECT
4. Double-click "Transmute.xcodeproj" in this folder. Xcode opens it.
5. In the left column, click the blue "Transmute" icon at the very top.
   In the middle, under TARGETS, click "Transmute", then the
   "Signing & Capabilities" tab.
6. Next to "Team", choose your name with "(Personal Team)".
   If Xcode then says the bundle identifier isn't available, change
   "Bundle Identifier" to something only you would use, for example
   com.yourname.transmute, and press Return.

PUT IT ON YOUR iPHONE (then repeat steps 7 to 11 for your iPad)
7. Connect the iPhone to the Mac with a cable and unlock it. If it asks
   "Trust This Computer?", tap Trust and enter your passcode.
8. Turn on Developer Mode on the iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Developer Mode (near the bottom) > On. The iPhone restarts; after it
   restarts, tap Turn On and enter your passcode.
   (Developer Mode only appears after the iPhone has been connected to Xcode.)
9. In Xcode, at the top of the window, click the device name next to
   "Transmute" and choose your iPhone from the list.
10. Click the Play button (top left) or press Command-R. The first build takes
    a few minutes. Keep the iPhone unlocked.
11. The first time, the iPhone says "Untrusted Developer". On the iPhone go to
    Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID under
    Developer App, then tap Trust. Now open Transmute from the Home Screen.

GOOD TO KNOW
- With a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days. Connect the device
  and press Play in Xcode again; your converted files stay.
- A free Apple ID can have up to 3 of these apps on a device at once.
- After the first time you can skip the cable: in Xcode choose Window >
  Devices and Simulators, select the iPhone and tick "Connect via network".
- Converted files are in the Files app under On My iPhone > Transmute > Converted.
- To send files to Transmute from another app, tap Share, then Transmute.
- Friends' iPhones: this needs the paid Apple Developer Program (about Rs 8,700
  a year). Then the app can go out through TestFlight, Apple's beta app, and
  friends install it from a link without a Mac.
